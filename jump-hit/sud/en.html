无间跳跃 — Privacy Notice

This notice explains how huang yu zhao, the developer of 无间跳跃, handles your personal information when the game runs on SUD OpenPaaS within a host app.

Information the developer handles

The game reads and stores your high score and your music, sound effects and vibration preferences on your device. This lets it display your personal best and restore your preferences the next time you play. The game does not send these records to the developer’s servers or require a separate game account, nickname, profile picture, phone number or national ID number.

If the host app supports vibration and you turn it on in the game settings, the game uses the host’s short vibration feature for feedback when you slam down. You can turn vibration off in the game settings.

If you email the developer, the developer uses your email address and the contents of your message to respond to, verify and handle your request. Please provide only what is needed to handle it.

Third-party platforms, plugins and SDKs

SUD OpenPaaS and the host app provide the environment and services that run the game. The SUD-GI SDK Privacy Policy, published by the SDK provider, 忽然科技（上海）有限公司, explains how the SDK handles information such as device brand and model for compatibility and troubleshooting. See the privacy policy of the host app you use and the SUD Privacy Policy for details.

If an ad placement is enabled in your version of the game and its host app, the game uses SUD’s ad interfaces to load or display rewarded video, interstitial or banner ads. The game sends the ad unit ID to the interface and receives status updates such as loading, closure or errors. If you choose to watch a rewarded video, the game uses the completion status returned by the platform to determine whether you can revive in the current round. See the privacy notices of your host app and the relevant ad provider for details about device information, ad interaction data and how they handle it.

How the developer stores information

Your high score and preferences stay in the game’s local storage within the current host app. Clearing the mini-game’s local data removes those records. The game has no cloud save from which to restore them.

Unless the law requires otherwise, the developer keeps contact details and correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your message and related requests. SUD, the host app and ad providers describe their own retention practices in their privacy notices.

How information is used and your rights

The developer uses the information described above for the purposes set out in this notice. If the purposes or methods of processing or the types of information change, the developer will tell you what changed and obtain consent again where applicable law requires it.

You can email huangyz192@gmail.com to request access to, a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to withdraw consent. For information and permissions managed by SUD, the host app or an ad provider, use their settings or contact channels.

Disclosure and developer contact

The developer does not sell your personal information. If it is necessary to provide personal information to another entity that processes personal information or disclose it publicly, the developer will inform you and obtain separate consent where applicable law requires it. The developer will provide information when required by law.

Minors should use the game with guidance from a parent or guardian. If the developer needs to process the personal information of a child under 14, the developer will obtain consent from the child’s guardian in advance and apply dedicated rules where required by law.

For questions, complaints or suggestions about this notice, or concerns about the protection of minors’ personal information, email huangyz192@gmail.com.
