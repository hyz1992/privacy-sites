Puzzle Time Privacy Notice

This notice is provided by huang yu zhao, the developer of Puzzle Time. It explains how information is handled when you play in SUD OpenPaaS or a host app.

What the game saves

The game reads and saves story unlocks and completion progress, unfinished boards, time remaining, item counts and preferences in the current host’s local game storage. This lets you resume puzzles and restore your settings. The game’s own code does not send these records to a developer server or require a separate account, nickname, avatar, phone number or identity document.

If vibration is enabled and supported by the host, the game uses the host’s short vibration feature for puzzle feedback. You can turn it off in the game settings.

If you contact the developer, your email address and the contents of your message are used to respond to and handle your request. The developer retains this information only as long as necessary for that purpose, unless the law requires otherwise.

Platform services and asset delivery

SUD and the host app run the mini game and deliver its assets. They may process device, network and diagnostic information under their respective privacy policies. For hosts that integrate the relevant SDK, the SUD-GI SDK privacy notice describes information used for compatibility and troubleshooting.

This notice is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page contains no analytics scripts. See the GitHub privacy statement for how the hosting service handles access data.

Local data and your rights

If your host lets you clear mini-game data, you can use that option to remove local progress and preferences. The developer does not provide cloud saves, so the game cannot restore those records after you clear them.

Email huangyz192@gmail.com to request access to, a copy of, correction of or deletion of personal information handled by the developer, to withdraw consent or to raise a privacy question or complaint. Use SUD’s or the host’s settings or contact channels for information they handle.

Children and updates

Minors should play with guidance from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 14 provided personal information to the developer without their guardian’s consent, please contact the developer so the issue can be checked and addressed.

If new features change how personal information is handled, the developer will update this notice and provide notice or seek consent when required by applicable law.
